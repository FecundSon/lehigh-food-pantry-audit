# Lehigh County Food Pantry Audit — DRAFT FOR KEITH'S ACCEPTANCE ONLY
**Date checked: 2026-10-24. Not sent to PA 211, Second Harvest, or any pantry.**
**Method:** public web search + page-text fetch only. No pantry, PA 211, or anyone
else was contacted. A pantry's own website is treated as authoritative over
third-party directories. A failed or missing page is reported as UNVERIFIED,
never as closure. No pantry in this report is called defunct.

## Headline findings (the ones worth sending on, once Keith accepts)
1. **Allentown Area Ecumenical Food Bank — address correction.**
   Directories/LVPM say 534 Chew St. The pantry's own site says
   **417 N. 14th Street, Allentown, PA 18102, (610) 821-1332**.
   Hours confirmed on own site: Mon–Fri 9am–noon, Thu 3:30–6pm.
   Source: allentownfoodbank.org.
2. **Cedar Crest Bible Fellowship Church — "weekly" is wrong.**
   Directories say weekly. The church's own site says the Food Bank is
   **by appointment, 1st and 3rd Thursdays, 6:30–8pm, call 610-432-2200**.
   A person turning up on a non-pantry week would find nothing.
   Source: cedarcrest.church/deacons.
3. **West Bethlehem Emergency Food Bank — last Wednesday is not closed.**
   Directories shorten this to "except last Wednesday." Own site:
   Wed 10am–noon, **last Wednesday 6–8pm** (evening session).
   Source: westbethfoodpantry.org.
4. **Betty Lou's Pantry, Coopersburg — address resolved, hours supplied.**
   Own site: **538 Thomas St., Coopersburg, PA 18036, 484-898-5482**;
   **2nd & 4th Saturdays, 8:30–10:30am (arrive by 10am)**.
   The competing 333 E. Oxford St. listing is an older/alternate directory
   variant, not the pantry's current stated address.
   Source: bettylouspantry.com.
5. **Two pilot items are NOT Lehigh County — drop or relabel.**
   - Living Christ Chapel Food Pantry: third-party address is Milford,
     **Pike County**, not Lehigh. Own site gives no pantry details.
   - Zion UCC Delly Berger Feed-A-Neighbor: third-party address is Lehighton,
     **Carbon County**, not Lehigh. Own-site candidate fetch failed once
     (not evidence of closure).
   Recommendation: remove both from a strict Lehigh County list, or relabel
   the pilot "Second Harvest service area." Do not present them as Lehigh pantries.

## What this report does NOT claim
- No pantry is reported as closed/defunct. Every "unverified" item above is
  a gap in public web evidence, not a finding of closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a pantry is currently operating.
- Hours from a church/office page are not pantry hours, and are flagged as
  such wherever that confusion was possible.
- The two out-of-county items are a scoping error in the pilot list (they
  came from a Second Harvest-network list, not a Lehigh-only list), not
  evidence about those pantries.
